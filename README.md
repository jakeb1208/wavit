# Wavit

**Project Status: Discontinued**

Wavit has been shut down and is no longer actively maintained. This repository is preserved as an archive of the project and its development.

Wavit was a SaaS platform designed to help service-based businesses manage queues and communicate live wait times to customers. The goal was to reduce uncertainty, improve customer experiences, and give businesses a simple way to manage customer flow without requiring additional hardware.

Website: https://www.wavit.cc

## Overview

Wavit aimed to replace outdated waiting-room and walk-in queue processes with a digital solution. Customers could join a queue through a QR code and view estimated wait times, while businesses could manage their queues through an administrative dashboard.

The platform was initially developed for barbershops and later explored for urgent care clinics and other service-based businesses.

## Features

- QR code queue joining for customers
- Live estimated wait times based on queue activity
- Queue management tools for businesses
- Embeddable waitlist widget for business websites
- Administrative dashboard for managing queues
- Multi-location support for businesses operating across multiple locations
- Customer data management, including removing customer information after a customer left the queue

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: Web-based user interface
- Backend and hosting: Railway
- Development: Replit AI
- Mobile application: Capacitor and Android Studio
- Domain: wavit.cc

## Project Background

Wavit was built to explore how software could improve everyday business operations by making waiting times more transparent and queue management more efficient.

Developing the platform provided practical experience in SaaS development, product design, customer discovery, sales outreach, deployment, and evaluating product-market fit.

## Project Status

Wavit is no longer operational. Development and business operations have been discontinued, and this repository is retained for portfolio purposes, documentation, and future reference.

The project represents an attempt to take a software idea from concept to a working product, test its potential with real businesses, and learn from the challenges of building and launching a SaaS startup.

Wavit — Less waiting. More certainty.
